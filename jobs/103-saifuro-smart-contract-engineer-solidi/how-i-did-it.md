# How this package was generated

- Source listing captured by `scripts/discover_jobs.py` from Crypto Jobs List.
- Fit score, keywords, headline, and summary written by local Ollama model `phi3:mini`.
- Resume built from `templates/resume-template.tex` with a role-specific headline and summary.
- Cover letter paragraphs generated by `phi3:mini` and inserted into `templates/cover-letter-template.tex`, compiled with pdflatex.
- Score: 85/100 (apply). Reasons: Strong match with relevant skills and experience in blockchain, smart contracts, and backend engineering, with a proactive approach to technology and a proven track record in hackathons and CTFs.
